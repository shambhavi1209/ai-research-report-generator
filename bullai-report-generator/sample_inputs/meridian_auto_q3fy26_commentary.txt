Meridian Auto Components Ltd — Q3 FY26 Results Commentary (Unaudited)
Sector: Auto Ancillaries | NSE: MERIDAUTO | BSE: 543912

Meridian Auto Components is a leading Indian manufacturer of precision transmission
and driveline components, supplying domestic OEMs and export markets across Europe
and North America from six manufacturing plants.

Q3 FY26 performance:
Revenue for the quarter grew 18.4% year on year to Rs 1,284 crore, well ahead of
industry volume growth of 9%, driven by new EV driveline orders and better export
realisations. EBITDA came in at Rs 190 crore, an EBITDA margin of 14.8%, up 140 bps
YoY on softer raw material costs and operating leverage. Adjusted PAT was Rs 104 crore.
Management has guided for an exit EBITDA margin above 15% for FY26.

The order book stands at Rs 4,100 crore, about 1.7x trailing twelve-month revenue,
of which 32% is from EV platforms. Net debt to EBITDA improved to 0.6x from 1.1x a
year ago. Capex of Rs 350 crore for the new Pune EV line is fully funded from
internal accruals. Exports contributed 41% of revenue this quarter.
